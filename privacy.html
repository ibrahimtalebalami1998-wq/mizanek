Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What Mizanek stores

  • Your account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • What you enter: profiles (books), people and their photos, debts, payments, monthly plans, payment proof files, and your settings.

Where it is kept

Your records are stored in your private account on our cloud database (Supabase), with a copy on your phone so the app works offline; changes sync through PowerSync. Security rules on the database let each account reach only its own records.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google, Mizanek receives only your name, email address and Google account ID, to sign you in. It does not read your contacts, Drive, Gmail or anything else in your Google account.

What we never do

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your data is never sold or shared. It is sent only to the services that run the app (Supabase and PowerSync), and sign-in codes are sent to you by email.

Backups and sharing

Backups are files saved on your own phone, optionally locked with your password. A statement or receipt is shared only when you choose to share it, with the app you pick.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → Delete my account and data removes your account and all your records and files from the cloud. Removing the app deletes the copy on your phone.
