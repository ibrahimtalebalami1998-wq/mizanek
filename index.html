Mizanek — your balance

Mizanek (ميزانك) keeps track of the money you owe and the money owed to you, person by person.

  • Record debts in both directions, payments and proof photos.
  • Monthly plans with reminders, and reports of what is left.
  • Works offline and syncs to your private account.
  • Share a PDF statement with the person concerned.

Sign in with your email and a password, or with Google. Your records are private to your account.
